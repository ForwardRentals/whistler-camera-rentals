FAQ

Common questions.

Where can I rent a camera in Whistler?

The kit on this page is owned by Whistler and Squamish photographer Jeremy Allen and booked through Rented Local. You can also browse cameras, lenses, drones and lighting listed by other Whistler locals on Rented Local.

Where do I pick the gear up?

This kit lives in Squamish, about 45 minutes south of Whistler on Highway 99. Pickup details are arranged when you book.

How much does a Canon R5 rental cost?

$150 CAD per day. Lenses start at $50 per day, the Godox AD600 Pro strobe is $60 per day and the RF to EF adapter is $10 per day. Live prices are always on Rented Local.

Can I use the EF lenses on an R5?

Yes. Add the RF to EF adapter and the 70-200 and Tamron 150-600 autofocus normally on the R5.

Can I hire a photographer instead?

Yes. Jeremy shoots events, conferences, real estate and adventure work across the Sea to Sky. See Whistler Conference Photography or The Full Time Hobby.
